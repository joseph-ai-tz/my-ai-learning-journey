# Lesson 02 : Prompt Engineering

## What is Prompt Engineering?

Prompt Engineering means giving AI clear instruction to get useful results.

## My practical Exercise

## What AI created

1. A short Advertisement.
2. A friendly WhatsApp message.
3. Three marketing ideas.
